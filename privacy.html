Information used by this website

When you create an account or place an order, the website uses details you provide such as your name, email address, mobile number, and delivery address. Order records also include the products, quantities, order total, payment status, and payment references needed to manage your purchase.

Accounts, orders, and payments

Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Firebase Storage provide the website’s account, order, and product-image services. Razorpay is intended to process checkout payments. Payment card numbers and CVV are not collected or stored by this website. Payment processing is unavailable until the showroom configures and tests its Firebase and Razorpay services.
