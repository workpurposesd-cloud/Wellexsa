Privacy policy.

Business review required before launch. Confirm the showroom’s final retention, contact, and privacy practices before publishing this policy.

Information used by this website

When you create an account or place an order, the website uses details you provide such as your name, email address, mobile number, and delivery address. Order records also include the products, quantities, order total, payment status, and payment references needed to manage your purchase.

Accounts, orders, and payments

Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Firebase Storage provide the website’s account, order, and product-image services. Razorpay is intended to process checkout payments. Payment card numbers and CVV are not collected or stored by this website. Payment processing is unavailable until the showroom configures and tests its Firebase and Razorpay services.

Showroom enquiries and follow-up

If you create a website account, the name, email address, and mobile number you provide may be copied to a private showroom customer-management system so authorized showroom staff can respond and record follow-up. A phone-number match is held for staff review and is not automatically merged with another customer record. Confirm the showroom’s final access, retention, and contact practices before publishing this policy.

Access and questions

Customer accounts are intended to show the account holder’s own orders. For questions about your information or an order, contact WELLEXSA MOTORS at 9989992230, 9121568577, or 9063182277.
